Privacy Policy

Effective 15 October 2026 · Bhoomiputra Tech Solutions Pvt Ltd, Bhiwandi, Maharashtra, India

Summary

BTS Creative is a desktop app that works on your own computer. We do not collect, store, sell or share your personal information, and the app has no accounts, ads or analytics.

Your files

PDFs, images, vector drawings, videos and projects you open or create stay on your device. They are never uploaded to our servers or to anyone else.

Network use

  • Microsoft Store version: the app does not connect to our servers. Updates are delivered by the Microsoft Store.
  • Website download version: the app checks creative.btspl.tech/updates/latest.yml to find new versions and downloads updates from GitHub. These requests contain only standard technical information (such as your IP address and app version) that web servers receive, which our host may keep in short-term server logs. We do not use it to identify you.

Data stored on your device

The app saves settings, a list of recently opened files and any signatures you choose to save, only on your computer. You can clear them by uninstalling the app.

Children

BTS Creative is made for students and is suitable for all ages. Because we collect no personal data, we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
